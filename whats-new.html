What’s new

Doodle Poker is not out yet. This is the list of its versions so far, taken from the version numbers the app has been given, newest first.

Since version 1.1.0

In the app now; not given a version number yet

  • Waiting for a seat: note when you arrive, sit down later, and see your average wait by place and by weekday.
  • Statistics: a bankroll graph, a calendar heat map, a Records tab with streaks and personal bests, My stats with questions you can add, and a “My year” review you can share as a picture.
  • Goals for hours, sessions or profit, shown on Home.
  • Planned sessions now show on Home as your next session, and you can start one from there.
  • Backup reminder: choose how many days (7 to start) on the Backup page. Home shows how long since your last backup once that many days have passed, and the phone sends a notification. Backup files now end in .doodlepoker (older .doodle-poker files still restore).
  • Lock the app with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.
  • Quotes said at the table, a bucket list of places and games, and session tags the app works out for you.
  • Find a feature: a search on Home that takes you to any setting or page.
Version 1.1.0

16 November 2026

The first numbered version.

  • Record hands on a drawn table, with side pots, rake, antes, straddles and replay, plus Quick Hand for typing a hand in shorthand.
  • Record sessions: buy-ins, top-ups, chips off, cash-outs, breaks, planned sessions and tournaments.
  • People with drawn faces, types, notes, ratings and spells.
  • The Doodle Book and Doodle Vault: a scrapbook for your poker.
  • Poker Ranges, the Range Game, the Equity Game and Spot check for studying, and Play Poker against the computer for play money.
  • Expenses, trips and a tax report as a PDF, and a home currency with exchange rates.
  • Backup and restore to a file, and a Bin that keeps deleted things for 30 days.
  • Optional Claude features with your own key: hand review, building a hand from words or a photo, reading receipts and importing files. Or copy a hand to any AI app.
