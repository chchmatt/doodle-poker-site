Questions and answers

Short answers about Doodle Poker. If yours is not here, ask.

When is it out, and for which phones?

It is not out yet, and we do not have a date to give you. It is being built for Android phones and iPhones. Check back here.

Does it need an internet connection?

Mostly no. Recording hands and sessions, people, places, stats, ranges, the practice games, Play Poker, the Doodle Book, backups and exports all work with no signal.

Three things use the internet: Claude features (only if you add your own key); exchange rates (the app looks up the rate between currencies for a day, if you have sessions in more than one currency; you can type rates yourself instead); and links that open your browser, such as adding a session to a web calendar.

Where is my data kept, and who can see it?

On your phone, and nowhere else. We do not run a server that collects it, and there are no accounts. Nobody but you can see it unless you choose to share something: a backup, an export, a picture or a hand. Your Claude key, if you add one, is kept in your phone’s secure store.

The privacy policy lists everything that can leave the phone.

What happens if I lose or replace my phone?

The app keeps everything on the phone, so without a backup file a lost phone means lost data. Make a backup file (Settings, then Backup and restore) and keep it somewhere safe, such as Drive or your email. On the new phone, install the app and restore from that file. By default the app reminds you to back up when it has been 14 days. See how to back up and restore.

Which games and formats does it handle?

No Limit Hold’em, in cash games and tournaments, with 2 to 10 players. Hands can have antes, straddles, rake, side pots and all-ins. Tournaments keep the buy-in, fee and starting chips. There is no Omaha, limit or stud.

Is it for online poker? Is real money involved?

It is a notebook for live games, played in a casino, card room or at home. You record the hands yourself. It does not take bets, hold money or connect to any poker site. The Play Poker game in the app is against computer players for play money only.

Can I use it at the table without slowing the game?

That is what it is built for. Quick Hand lets you type a hand in shorthand afterwards. The quick money button handles top-ups and cash-outs in a tap or two. “Quiet at a live table” silences card and chip sounds. You can hide the amounts and your hole cards when a session starts, and the screen hides when you leave the app.

Can I bring in data from another tracker, or get my data out?

Out: hands as a spreadsheet (CSV) or as text hand histories in the PokerStars layout; sessions and expenses as spreadsheets; expenses, trips and the tax report as PDF; hands, people and your year as pictures; and a full backup file. We have not tested the text histories in other tracker programs, so we cannot promise they will import there.

In: there is no ready-made importer for any one program. Instead you choose files (spreadsheets, PDFs, pictures, CSV or text) and Claude reads them and sorts them into sessions, places, people, expenses and hands. You tick what to bring in, and you can undo it. This needs your own Claude key and an internet connection. You can also paste a PokerStars-layout hand history into a hand with no key.

How does the “Ask Claude” hand review work, and what does it send?

It is optional and off until you paste in your own Claude API key (Settings, Claude API). The app then sends the hand straight from your phone to Anthropic, the company that makes Claude, at api.anthropic.com. It goes only when you tap the button. We never see it.

What is sent: the blinds and ante, the number of players and the currency sign; each player’s position, the name as you typed it, the tags and notes you wrote about that person and in this hand, their starting stack, and the hole cards that are known; any links between players; every action with the pot; the board, the result, the rake and the payouts; the hand’s tags and your notes; the meaning of any of your own abbreviations used in the notes; and the app’s instructions to Claude.

What is not sent: the place, the session, the date and time, your photos, your bankroll or any other hand.

Player names are sent as typed, and there is no switch to hide them. If you do not want a name to go, change it in the hand first.

Your key is sent only to Anthropic. You can send a hand as a batch, which is cheaper and answered later. The answer is kept on the hand, on your phone.

No key? Copy for any AI puts the same text on your clipboard, or you can share it to an AI app of your choice. What that app does with it is up to that app.

The same key is used for the other optional Claude features (building a hand from words or a photo of the table, reading a receipt, importing files, drawing a face from a description). They send only what each needs, and the privacy policy lists them.

Which currencies does it support?

A session can use any currency. Twenty-eight common signs are built in, from the dollar, pound, euro and yen to the Swiss franc, Brazilian real and South African rand, and you can enter any other sign with its three-letter code. A home currency adds everything up in one currency, using the rate for each day. Rates are looked up for you, or you can type them in. If no rate can be found, type one.

Can I stop someone else looking at my notes?

Yes. You can lock the app with your fingerprint, face or phone screen lock. The screen can hide when you leave the app, and you can hide amounts and your hole cards at the table.

Is it in other languages?

English only, for now.

How do I report a problem or suggest something?

Email support@doodlepoker.com. Tell us what phone you have and what happened. The Support page has the same address.
