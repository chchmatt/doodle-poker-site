Waiting, travel and the door-to-door rate

Built Pro · for full-time players

A session can say how long you waited for a seat and how long it took to get there and home. The wait is timed with the Waiting for a seat button. Getting there and Getting home are minutes you type (25, 1h 05) or time with Leaving now, Arrived and Home now. A place can offer your usual travel from the last five sessions there, but it only fills in what is empty.

On the Results tab, under An hour, a second figure shows your result an hour door to door: the result over the time at the table plus the wait plus the travel. It appears only when some session has a wait or travel. The By place and By stakes lines add it too, and a switch, Rank by door to door, can order By place by it.

At the foot of Hero’s stats are the waiting averages: the average, shortest and longest wait, your rate with and without the wait, and the average by place and by day of the week. A wait of 45 minutes or more suggests the tag Long wait for a seat.
