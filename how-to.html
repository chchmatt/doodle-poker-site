How to…

Four short guides for the jobs you will do most. The names of buttons are written as they appear in the app.

  • Record a hand
  • Track a session
  • Add a person
  • Back up and restore

Record a hand

Do it as it happens, or afterwards. A hand saves itself as you go, so you can leave and come back.

  1. On Home, tap HANDS, then Record a hand. (In a session, use the buttons on the session page.)
  2. Set the number of players and tap your own seat on the table.
  3. Tap a seat, then the pad, to add each action: fold, check, call, bet, raise or all in. Deal the cards as they come.
  4. Made a slip? Back one step takes back the last action.
  5. When the hand is over, the app works out the pot, the side pots and the winners.
  6. Use the tabs on the hand (Hand, Actions, Notes and AI) to add notes and tags. Tap Share this hand to send it as text or a picture.

In a hurry? Tap QUICK HAND on Home and type the hand in your own shorthand. Typing and saving work with no signal. You can keep it as a note, or (with your own Claude key) have the app build the hand from your words.

Track a session

From sitting down to cashing out.

  1. On Home, tap SESSIONS, then the plus button.
  2. Choose a place (you must pick one), the game, the currency and your buy-in. Check the start time.
  3. Tap Start now. Home now shows PLAYING and a stopwatch. If you are waiting for a seat, tap Waiting for a seat instead, and start the session when you sit down.
  4. Use the quick money button at the bottom left of Home to Top up, Set stack, take Chips off or Cash out, and to go on a break.
  5. When you are done, cash out and end the session. You can rate it, tag it and add notes.

Planning ahead? Set the start time in the future and save it as a planned session. You can add it to your phone’s calendar, and Home shows it as your next session.

Add a person

Only a name is needed. Add the rest when you have it.

  1. On Home, tap PEOPLE, then the plus button.
  2. Type a name. If you do not know it, switch on Random person.
  3. Pick a type if you like, such as TAG, nit or fish.
  4. Give them a face: build one from parts, or describe them in words.
  5. Add notes and a rating, then tap Add this person.

From a hand: the player boxes in a hand have Save to my people, so you can add someone without leaving the hand.

Back up and restore

Everything lives on your phone, so a backup file is your safety net. Make one now and then, and keep it somewhere other than the phone.

  1. On Home, tap the gear for Settings, then Backup and restore.
  2. Choose what to include. Everything is ticked except your Claude key.
  3. Tap Backup. Your phone’s share sheet opens: save the file to Drive, email it to yourself, or send it to your computer. It is named backup- and the date, ending .doodle-poker.
  4. To restore (on this phone or a new one): install Doodle Poker, open Backup and restore and tap Restore from a file, then pick your file. (On a phone with the app installed, opening the file from Drive or email does the same.)
  5. The app shows what is in the file next to what is on the phone. Choose Add what is missing to keep everything already there, or Replace to make the ticked parts match the file.

Your Claude key is left out of a backup unless you switch on “Include my Claude key”. The app reminds you to back up after 14 days by default; you can change that in Settings.
