Pricing

Doodle Poker has three tiers: Rec for casual play, Reg for regular players and Pro for full-time players. Each tier has everything in the tiers before it. Which feature is in which tier is on the Features page.

These prices are planned and may change. Nothing can be bought yet. Prices are in US dollars; an app store may show a local price.

Rec and Reg are poker words for a casual player and a regular, and Pro is a full-time player. The names say what a tier is for, not what anyone is.
