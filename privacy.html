Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy is for the Doodle Poker app for Android and iOS, and for this website. It is made by the Doodle Poker team (“we”, “us”).

The short version

Doodle Poker keeps what you record on your phone. We do not collect, store or share your personal information. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no tracking. Things leave your phone only in the cases listed below, and each one happens because you asked for it, except the exchange-rate lookup, which the app does by itself and which carries no personal information.

What the app keeps, on your device only

  • the hands, sessions, expenses, trips, places and people you record, with their notes and drawn faces;
  • photos you take or pick (of hands, receipts or Doodle Book pages) and the Doodle Book itself;
  • your settings, your goals and your practice results;
  • things you delete, kept in the Bin for 30 days;
  • if you add one, your Claude key, in your phone’s secure store.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. It stays on your device until you delete it, uninstall the app or clear its data.

Optional: publishing a hand

In the app you can choose Share, then doodlepoker.com on a hand. Nothing is uploaded unless you do. Then the hand, as you chose to publish it (you choose the names, which notes, tags, pictures and AI reviews go with it, and names can be replaced by “Villain 1” and so on), and, when there is one, the picture of its table, are sent to a small service we run and are stored on Microsoft Azure servers in the United States. The hand is then visible to anyone who has its link until you take it down in the app (Settings, My published hands), or until the end of the time you chose. Its address is long and random so it cannot be guessed, the page is not listed on search engines, and we do not link to it.

To tell your hand from other people’s, the app makes a secret key once and keeps it in your phone’s secure store. It is sent with each publish and unpublish, and we keep only a one-way scrambled copy of it (a hash) next to the hand; it is not part of a backup and is not linked to your name, email or any account. We also count how many hands an owner and an internet address publish in a day, to stop abuse; the address is kept only as a scrambled copy and only for a day or two. Microsoft, as our host, may keep ordinary server logs. We do not use what you publish for anything else, and if you or anyone reports a hand to support@doodlepoker.com we may take it down.

Optional: Claude

The app can use Claude, made by Anthropic. It is off until you paste in your own Claude API key in Settings. Nothing is sent to Anthropic before that. After that, each time you tap a button that uses Claude, what the feature needs goes from your phone straight to Anthropic (api.anthropic.com), using your key. We never receive it. What Anthropic does with it is covered by Anthropic’s privacy policy.

  • Hand review (live, or as a batch that Anthropic answers in its own time and the app collects later): the blinds, ante, number of players and currency sign; each player’s position, the name you typed, the tags and notes you wrote about them, their starting stack and known hole cards; links between players; every action; the board, the result, the rake and payouts; the hand’s tags and your notes; and the meaning of any of your own abbreviations used in them. Not sent: the place, the session, the date and time, your photos, your bankroll or any other hand. Names are sent as typed, and there is no switch to hide them.
  • Building or fixing a hand from words (the hand, and your words).
  • Building a hand from a photo of a table (the photos you choose, and your shorthand notes).
  • Reading a receipt (the receipt photo).
  • Importing data (the whole files you choose, and your notes).
  • Describing a face or an item (the words you type).
  • Ranges, stats and the Doodle Book “Ask Claude” (what you type and a summary of the thing you are asking about).
  • Estimating an exchange rate (two currency codes and a date).

The key is kept in your phone’s secure store and is sent only to Anthropic. It is left out of backups unless you switch on “Include my Claude key” when you make one. A batch that is still waiting for its answer is kept on the hand (its number, so the app can ask Anthropic for it), and is in your backups. The answers are kept on your phone.

You can also use any AI app without a key: the app copies the same text as a review sends to your clipboard, or hands it to the share sheet, and you paste it into the AI app of your choice. What that app does with it is covered by its own privacy policy.

Exchange rates

If you have sessions or expenses in more than one currency, the app looks up the rate between them for a day from a free service, api.frankfurter.app. This happens when the app opens and when you ask for it on the Home currency page. Only the currency codes and the date are sent. The service sees your phone’s internet address, as any website does. You can turn to typing the rates yourself instead.

Calendar, browser and phone links

  • When you add a planned session to your calendar, the app writes it to your phone’s calendar, or opens Google, Outlook or Yahoo Calendar in your browser, as you choose. The event contains a link to doodlepoker.com/open/, a page on this website that only passes you back to the app; it does not carry anything else.
  • When you tap a place’s queue number, your phone starts a call or opens its message app with a text ready. You send it.

Backups, exports and sharing

Backups, exports (spreadsheets, text, PDF) and “share” (as text or as a picture) are made only when you ask. They are files or text handed to your device’s share sheet or clipboard, and where they go is up to you. The app never sends them anywhere itself.

What the app asks your phone for

Each of these is asked for only when needed, and used on your phone:

  • Camera and photos: when you take or pick a photo for a hand, a receipt or the Doodle Book. Photos are read only when you pick them.
  • Files: when you choose a backup to restore or files to import.
  • Calendar: to add a planned session when you ask.
  • Notifications: reminders the phone itself shows (breaks, stack updates, missions). No server is involved.
  • Fingerprint, face or screen lock: only to unlock the app, if you switch the lock on. The app never sees your fingerprint or face.
  • Vibration, motion and battery level: for feel, for pictures that swing when you tilt the phone, and to slow the animation on a low battery. These stay on the phone.

The app does not use your location, contacts or microphone. Apart from what is listed above, the only things it sends are those you ask for.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no scripts from anyone else, and its fonts are served from the site itself. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any host, may keep ordinary server logs; we do not receive them.

The app stores

When you download the app from Google Play or the App Store, Google or Apple may collect information under their own privacy policies. We do not receive any personal information from them.

Changes

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if a new feature sends data to a service: the publishing of a hand above is such a feature), this policy will be updated first, and the date above changed.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@doodlepoker.com.
